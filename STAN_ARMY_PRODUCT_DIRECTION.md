# STAN ARMY — Product Direction Clarification

User-approved guiding direction, November 7, 2026. This broadens earlier music/artist-only assumptions without replacing the fan-led structure, customisation rules or visual-review gate.

**Whatever you stan for. Stan for it here.**

Although we're launching with music fandoms because they're a natural starting audience, Stan Army is ultimately a platform for **anything people love**.

An Army could celebrate Britney Spears, a football club, a film, a fashion designer, or even Brie cheese.

## What this means for the product

- **Keep the existing flexible Army structure.** We don't need category-specific templates.
- **Make content modules optional.** Music players, video boxes, galleries, chat and other features should be independently enabled or hidden by the Army's authorised customiser.
- **Don't assume every Army is about music.** Avoid mandatory artist biographies, discographies or music-specific fields.
- **Keep Spotify available to everyone.** Any Army can feature a playlist, regardless of its subject.
- **Preserve existing customisation, permissions and functionality.** Don't disrupt the current Nova prototype.

## Launch strategy

Music remains our initial focus for discovery, onboarding examples and marketing. We don't need to populate the platform with dozens of unrelated categories yet.

But the underlying experience should never prevent people from creating an Army around something else.

## Scope

Treat this as a guiding product principle for current and future development.

Where practical, make existing media modules optional within the customisation controls. Don't undertake a major rebuild or add unnecessary complexity.

**The platform provides the tools. The stans decide what to do with them.**
