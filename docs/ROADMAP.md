# Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — visual review gate

Foundation docs, Next.js scaffold, polished artist room, landing/discovery and creation wizard with explicit demo content. Run lint/typecheck/unit tests/build. Review at desktop and mobile sizes, recover original logo, fine-tune visual density, imagery and artist personality. Stop before backend work.

## Milestone 2 — best next engineering milestone, after design review

Accounts and persistent pages: select/price auth and storage, internal identity, passwordless login and supported social providers, secure linking, profiles, artist ownership and invited manager roster permissions. Introduce migrations/RLS, validated links and controlled media uploads, wizard save/edit/publish, and idempotent real enlistment. Test own/other-user and cross-artist access at the server and database levels. No charges or production users without approval.

## Milestone 3 — community

One persistent realtime chatroom per artist, durable messages/timestamps/reactions, pinned announcements, report/mute/delete/ban, rate limits, reconnection and recovery. Posts with real public/enlisted visibility. Load-test realistic room concurrency and document scaling/costs. Confirm moderation workflow and retention before live use.

## Milestone 4 — discovery and reporting

Real search and editorial curation (never paid favourites), truthful engagement counters, manager reports and artist dashboards, mobile/accessibility refinement, reliable loading/error recovery and monitoring. No invented streaming counts or royalties.

## Milestone 5 — separate approval required

Battleground polls from approved catalogue links, anti-duplicate voting and premium artist entitlements; free fan participation. Stripe test-mode checkout/cancellation, webhook replay/idempotency and entitlement tests before any live billing. Provisional configurable Pro $19/month, Elite $59/month, one-month trial and selected three-month founding promotions remain unconfirmed business assumptions. Clarify refunds before launch. Never claim synchronized cross-user playback unless provider support and permission are established.

## Confirmed boundaries

No production deployment, paid services, real payments or live user data without explicit approval. Design-direction approval is not backend approval. Fine-tune the prototype with user feedback before moving on.
